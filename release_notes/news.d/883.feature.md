Condition-combination coverage: `--cov-kind=combination` counts each combination of the input values of conditions and logic expressions (an operand a short-circuit skips is "not evaluated"), and a comment such as `-- yavs cov exclude 01: impossible handshake` or `-- yavs coverage exclude condition (valid=false, ready=true)` waives a combination you know cannot occur. The comments are checked when the sources are analysed, with a suggestion for a misspelt input, `--coverage-directives=ignore` reads none of them, and `yavs cov report --exclusions` (also `=json`, and a page in the HTML report) lists every exclusion rule, in the source or in an exclusion file, with what it matched.
