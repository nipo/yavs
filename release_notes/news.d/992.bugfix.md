A process without a label is now called by what it is and where, such as `tb.clk <= ... (tb.vhd:17)`, in reports, run-time errors, `--print-hierarchy`, `--activity`, `--profile`, `--hang-report`, the console and the debugger, instead of an internal name such as `tb._p0`. Variable paths, wave scopes and VHPI keep the internal name, since they need one token.
