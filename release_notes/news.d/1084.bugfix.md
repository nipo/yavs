A statement label with the name of an implicit predefined operation of its region, such as `minimum : process` next to `type t is (u, v)`, is accepted again, as LRM 2008 12.3 says, and hides the operation; a call of the operation after the label is an error. `fifo : fifo` with the component made visible by a use clause is still error `E1700`, which now says that the label hides the component and asks for the label to be renamed.
