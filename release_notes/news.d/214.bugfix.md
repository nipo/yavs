Correction: yavs 0.4.0 to 0.6.0 accepted the literal `-9223372036854775808` (and `-9223372036854775808 fs`) as the 64-bit minimum. That was a bug: the standard makes the literal an error before the minus applies (LRM 9.5, 9.3.6), and it has been rejected since 0.7.0. The error now names the literal and says to write `-9223372036854775807 - 1` (`-9223372036854775807 fs - 1 fs` for a physical type).
