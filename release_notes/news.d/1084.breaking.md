A statement label that repeats a declaration of its own declarative region is now error `E1101` (LRM 2008 10.1, 11.1, 12.1, 12.3: a label is declared in the enclosing process, subprogram, block, generate, architecture or entity, and two homographs may not be declared in one region). This covers a sequential label against a variable, constant, type, subprogram or parameter of its process or subprogram, even inside a loop; a concurrent label (process, block, generate, instance, assertion, assignment, procedure call) against any declaration of its architecture, block or generate, a port, a generic or a generate parameter; and a label in an entity's statement part against a declaration or a label of its architecture. Rename one of the two. A homograph in a nested region still only hides, so a signal of the architecture and a label in one of its processes or blocks may share a name. There is no `--relaxed` for this.
